Questions for

Write three questions only they could answer. Their answers become their key to your vault, and we never see them. Any two correct answers will open it. Avoid anything on social media, birthdays and pets' names.

Capitals, spaces, accents and punctuation never matter. Add other ways they might say an answer, separated by commas. Pick answers nobody could guess or look up online.
