Save My Secrets

Questions only your nominee can answer

Your nominee won't need a card or a code. Instead, when they come to us, we'll ask them a few questions only they would know. They need to get 2 of the 3 right.

Good questions are things that aren't on social media and that family members wouldn't all know. Avoid birthdays, pets' names and your mother's maiden name.

Talking to Save My Secrets

This is the part of the conversation where your nominee answers your questions. Before this, they've given their name and email and told our assistant what has happened.

What happens next

1. If you asked for one, the death certificate is uploaded and checked.
2. A person at Save My Secrets reviews everything.
3. You get a final window, with alerts by email, text and WhatsApp, to say "I'm fine" and stop it.
4. Only then is the vault opened for your nominee.
