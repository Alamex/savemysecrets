Save My Secrets

Questions only your nominees can answer

Your nominees won't need a card or a code. Each nominee gets their own three questions, ones only they would know, and needs to get 2 of the 3 right. If one nominee forgets, another can still open your vault. This preview shows the questions for one nominee.

Good questions are things that aren't on social media and that family members wouldn't all know. Avoid birthdays, pets' names and your mother's maiden name.

Only exact answers, or the other ways of saying it that you list, will work. Capitals, spaces, accents and punctuation are ignored. Your answers are never sent to us: they become the key itself.

Talking to Save My Secrets

This is the part of the conversation where one of your nominees answers the questions you set for them. Before this, they've given their name and email and told our assistant what has happened.

What happens next

1. If you asked for one, the death certificate is uploaded and checked.
2. Requests are assessed through our claims process, which includes an AI interview and automated checks, and we may contact you by email.
3. One final check that you don't respond, with alerts by email. One tap from you stops it.
4. Only then is the vault opened for them.
