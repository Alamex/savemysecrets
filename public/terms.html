This is an alpha

The service is still being built and tested. Features will change, things may break, and we may need to reset accounts. Do not store anything mission critical, and keep your own copies of everything you put in your vault.

Until you set questions for every nominee, we hold a release key so we can pass your vault on. We explain what that means on our privacy page.

Your side

  • Keep your master password safe, and if you use two-factor login (which we strongly recommend), your two-factor app and recovery codes too. We cannot reset your master password. If you lose it, we cannot get your vault back for you.
  • Give us accurate details for yourself and your nominees, and only name people you are happy for us to contact.
  • Respond to check-ins. If you don't, we will follow up with you and then contact your nominees, as described on the homepage.
  • Tell your nominees, for example in your will or a letter, to visit savemysecrets.com when the time comes.
  • Only store things you have the right to pass on, and don't use the service for anything unlawful.

Nominees

If you ask for access to a vault, you must be the person named and tell the truth. Requests are assessed through our claims process, which includes an AI interview and automated checks, and we may contact you by email. Trying to get into someone else's vault is fraud and we will report it. We contact the owner as soon as a request starts, and one response from them closes it.

Release decisions

We release a vault only when the checks the owner chose have been passed and the owner has not responded within their final check period. Requests are assessed through our claims process, which includes an AI interview and automated checks, and we may contact you by email. We can't promise these checks will always reach the right outcome, and a vault may be released if an owner is unable to respond for reasons other than death. Choose your check-in and follow-up periods with that in mind.

If nominees can't get in

Once every nominee has questions, a vault can only be opened with their answers. If none of your nominees can answer, the vault cannot be recovered, by anyone, including us. That is deliberate.
