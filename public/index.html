How it works

  1. 1

    Lock it away

    Add personal messages, logins you want to pass on, account details and documents. Everything is encrypted before it leaves your phone or laptop. Log in from any device, with two-factor login as standard.

  2. 2

    Name your people

    Choose who should receive it and add their details, so we can check it's really them. They hear nothing from us until the day it matters.

  3. 3

    Set their questions

    Write three questions for each of your nominees, ones only they could answer. Each nominee's answers become their own key to your vault, and we never see them.

  4. 4

    We check in

    Every few months, one tap to say you're fine. Miss it and we follow up with you by email for weeks before anyone else is contacted.

  5. 5

    Verified hand-over

    Our AI interviews your nominees to understand what has happened. Then come your proof rules, such as a death certificate. After automated checks and a final waiting period, only then do their answers open your vault, on their own devices.

Only your nominees can open it.

No cards, no codes

Your nominees don't need a password or a key. Leave a message in your will or a letter telling them to visit savemysecrets.com, and our verification assistant takes it from there. We also email your nominees when the time comes, so they don't have to rely on finding your note.

Their answers are the key

Your vault opens only with answers your nominees know plus our automated release after checks. Neither works alone, and we never see the answers. Once every nominee has questions, we delete our own copy, so we can't open your vault without their answers.

Who they are, checked

Before anything is released, we check your nominees are who they say they are: an AI interview to understand what has happened, plus automated checks and a waiting period.

Why we can't see your data.

Locked on your device

When you save, your phone or laptop encrypts everything using a key made from your master password. That password never reaches us, so all we receive is encrypted data we can't read.

Your nominees' keys, not ours

Each nominee's copy is locked with a key made from their own answers to your questions. They type and check the answers on their own device, and the answers are never sent to us.

Check it yourself

Every line of our code is public on GitHub, so anyone can confirm that what's in your vault is encrypted before it leaves your device.

Alpha note: until you've set questions for every nominee, we hold a copy of your vault key ourselves. Choose answers nobody could guess or look up: anyone who stole our database could try guessing them.

Questions

What if my nominees forget their answers?

Each nominee has their own questions, so if one forgets, another can still open your vault. Each needs two of their three answers, and capitals, spaces and accents don't matter. You can also list other ways they might say it. Every check-in reminds you to make sure your questions still make sense. But if none of your nominees can answer, no one can open your vault, including us. That's what keeps it private.

Can you see what's in my vault?

Your vault is encrypted before it leaves your phone or laptop, and once every nominee has questions, the key to open it is made from answers we never see. All our code is published on GitHub so you can see exactly what we store and how the encryption works.

What if I'm in hospital or travelling?

You choose how often we check in (monthly to yearly) and how long we keep trying you (2 to 12 weeks). Before anything is released we make one final check that you don't respond, with email alerts. One tap stops everything.

What can I store?

Anything you'd want someone to have if you weren't here: passwords, bank and pension details, where documents are kept, instructions, a letter.

What stops someone pretending to be one of my nominees?

They'd have to pass an AI interview, automated checks, your proof rules (such as a death certificate), and a final waiting period. Even then, without a nominee's answers, the vault stays locked. Every attempt is logged, and repeated wrong answers mean longer waits.

Is it ready for my important information?

Not yet. Save My Secrets is in alpha: we're testing it with a small group of approved users, so please don't store anything mission critical yet and keep your usual backups. Your feedback shapes the full launch.
